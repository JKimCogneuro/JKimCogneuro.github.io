Biography

I received my Ph.D. degree in Psychology under the supervision of Dr. Eunjoo Kang at Kangwon National University in South Korea. During my Ph.D., I examined the neuropsychological mechanism of reward processing during learning in individuals with Internet gaming disorder using functional MRI.

From January 2016 to October 2019, I was a postdoctoral fellow in Dr. Antonio Strafella’s lab at the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health, where I studied neuroimaging biomarkers of Parkinson’s disease. In particular, my postdoctoral research focused on identifying abnormalities in brain connectivity using resting-state fMRI and PET.

I am currently a Research Professor at the Social & Decision Neuroscience Lab, Korea University, directed by Dr. Hackjin Kim. My research focuses on the neurobehavioral mechanisms of social evaluation and self-esteem regulation, integrating wearable physiology, ecological momentary assessment (EMA), and neuroimaging approaches.

Interests

  • Social evaluation & self-esteem regulation
  • Interoception & brain–body dynamics
  • Wearables & EMA for digital mental health
